2. Sentinel-2 imagery and water class

The *_s2.tif files contain modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data. They were
cropped to fixed tiles and resampled to a common 10 m grid. The water class in
*_mask.tif is derived from the same data, using NDWI and the Sentinel-2 Scene
Classification Layer. In scenes from November to February, water was kept
only where a March to October scene of the same place shows water too, or
cannot see the ground.

The authors do not relicense this data. It is governed by the Legal Notice on
the use of Copernicus Sentinel Data and Service Information:
https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice

Anyone who distributes this data or makes it public must include this notice:

  Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020-2026.


3. Burn classes and the EFFIS export

The fresh burn, old burn and older burn classes in *_mask.tif are adapted from
burnt-area perimeters published by the European Forest Fire Information System
(EFFIS) of the Copernicus Emergency Management Service.

  Source: https://forest-fire.emergency.copernicus.eu/
  Copyright: © European Union, 1995-2026
  Licence: CC BY 4.0, as stated at
  https://forest-fire.emergency.copernicus.eu/about-effis/data-license

Changes made to the EFFIS data:

  - perimeters were reprojected and rasterised to each tile's 10 m grid,
    using every perimeter that touches the tile and is dated before the scene
  - burned pixels were split by the days between the recorded fire date and
    the satellite acquisition date: fresh (up to 90 days), old (more than 90
    and up to 365 days) and older (more than 365 days, only where the pixel
    would otherwise be clear)
  - burned pixels were merged with the other mask classes, with cloud, cloud
    shadow and nodata taking priority over burn, and burn over water

The Zenodo record also holds the EFFIS export that the burn classes were made
from (effis_export_2026-08-04.zip), unchanged and with its own readme. Its
terms are the EFFIS terms above.

As a cautious measure, because EFFIS is a Copernicus service, distributions
should also include this notice:

  Contains modified Copernicus Service information 2020-2026.
