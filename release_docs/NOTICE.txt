Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020-2026.
Contains modified Copernicus Service information 2020-2026.

Burn labels adapted from the European Forest Fire Information System (EFFIS),
Copernicus Emergency Management Service, © European Union, 1995-2026, licensed
under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes:
perimeters rasterised to a 10 m grid, split into fresh, old and older burn
classes by date, and merged with cloud, cloud shadow and water classes.

Cloud and cloud shadow classes predicted with OmniCloudMask (MIT-licensed
software): https://github.com/DPIRD-DMA/OmniCloudMask
